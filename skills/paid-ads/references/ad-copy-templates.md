## Primary Text Formulas

### 问题-Agitate-Solve (PAS)

```
[Problem statement]
[Agitate the pain]
[Introduce solution]
[CTA]
```

**示例：**
> Spending hours on manual reporting every week?
> While you're buried in spreadsheets, your competitors are making decisions.
> [产品] automates your reports in minutes.
> Start your free trial →

---

### Before-After-Bridge (BAB)

```
[Current painful state]
[Desired future state]
[Your product as the bridge]
```

**示例：**
> Before: Chasing down approvals across email, Slack, and spreadsheets.
> After: Every approval tracked, automated, and on time.
> [产品] connects your tools and keeps projects moving.

---

### 社会认同 Lead

```
[Impressive stat or testimonial]
[What you do]
[CTA]
```

**示例：**
> "We cut our reporting time by 75%." — Sarah K., 营销 Director
> [产品] automates the reports you hate building.
> See how it works →

---

### Feature-收益 Bridge

```
[Feature]
[So that...]
[Which means...]
```

**示例：**
> Real-time collaboration on documents
> So your team always works from the latest version
> Which means no more version confusion or lost work

---

### Direct Response

```
[Bold claim/outcome]
[Proof point]
[CTA with urgency if genuine]
```

**示例：**
> Cut your reporting time by 80%
> Join 5,000+ 营销 teams already using [产品]
> Start free → First month 50% off

## 标题 Formulas

### For 搜索 Ads

| Formula | 示例 |
|---------|---------|
| [关键词] + [收益] | "Project Management That Teams Actually Use" |
| [Action] + [Outcome] | "Automate Reports \| Save 10 Hours Weekly" |
| [Question] | "Tired of Manual Data Entry?" |
| [Number] + [收益] | "500+ Teams Trust [产品] for [Outcome]" |
| [关键词] + [Differentiator] | "CRM Built for Small Teams" |
| [Price/Offer] + [关键词] | "Free Project Management \| No Credit Card" |

### For Social Ads

| 类型 | 示例 |
|------|---------|
| Outcome hook | "How we 3x'd our 转化率" |
| Curiosity hook | "The reporting hack no one talks about" |
| Contrarian hook | "Why we stopped using [常见 tool]" |
| 具体性 hook | "The exact template we use for..." |
| Question hook | "What if you could cut your admin time in half?" |
| Number hook | "7 ways to improve your 工作流 today" |
| Story hook | "We almost gave up. Then we found..." |

## CTA Variations

### Soft CTAs (awareness/consideration)

Best for: Top of funnel, cold audiences, complex products

- Learn More
- See How It Works
- Watch Demo
- Get the Guide
- Explore 特性
- See 示例
- Read the Case Study

### Hard CTAs (conversion)

Best for: Bottom of funnel, warm audiences, clear offers

- Start Free Trial
- Get Started Free
- Book a Demo
- Claim Your Discount
- Buy Now
- Sign Up Free
- Get Instant Access

### Urgency CTAs (use when genuine)

Best for: Limited-time offers, 稀缺性 situations

- Limited Time: 30% Off
- Offer Ends [Date]
- Only X Spots Left
- Last Chance
- Early Bird Pricing Ends Soon

### Action-Oriented CTAs

Best for: Active voice, clear next step

- Start Saving Time Today
- Get Your Free Report
- See Your Score
- Calculate Your ROI
- Build Your First Project

## 平台-Specific 文案 Guidelines

### Google 搜索 Ads

- **标题 limits:** 30 characters each (up to 15 headlines)
- **说明 limits:** 90 characters each (up to 4 descriptions)
- Include 关键词 naturally
- Use all available 标题 slots
- Include numbers and stats when possible
- Test dynamic 关键词 insertion

### Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)

- **Primary text:** 125 characters visible (can be longer, gets truncated)
- **标题:** 40 characters recommended
- Front-load the hook (first line matters most)
- Emojis can work but test
- Questions perform well
- Keep image text under 20%

### LinkedIn Ads

- **Intro text:** 600 characters max (150 recommended)
- **标题:** 200 characters max (70 recommended)
- Professional tone (but not boring)
- Specific job outcomes resonate
- Stats and 社会认同 important
- Avoid consumer-style hype

## 文案 测试 Priority

When 测试 ad 文案, focus on these elements in order of impact:

1. **Hook/angle** (biggest impact on 表现)
2. **标题**
3. **Primary 收益**
4. **CTA**
5. **Supporting 证明材料**

Test one element at a time for clean data.
